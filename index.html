Knight of Santiago
Contents
Background
Honorem Immaculatum"Unblemished honor"Motto of the Order of Santiago after its refounding as a Military Order

The Order of Santiago was the third Military Order founded in PanOceania. Its mission is to protect pilgrims as they follow the different pilgrimage routes from any PanOceanian system to the holy places of Earth.

A great number of PanOceanian citizens use the commercial lines covered by the Circulars to make pilgrimages to the holy places of Earth and Neoterra. The Order of Santiago, at the behest of PanOceania and O-12, assumes the task of providing and coordinating aid and protection to the pilgrims and the Circulars that travel the pilgrimage routes, as it would do in ancient times.

The Knights of Santiago are also responsible for PanOceanian space transport security. They are part of the security detachments deployed on the Circulars by Starmada, the naval division of O-12's Bureau Aegis, to serve as military attachés under international command, and the members of this Order share control of security with Bureau Aegis on some of the Circular lines which tie PanOceanian systems to Earth.

The Order of Santiago usually works side by side with the PanOceanian Navy, assimilated into their crews or with their own attack and boarding ships, easily recognized by the red cross of Santiago stamped on their sides. Within the PanOceanian Military Complex, the Knights of Santiago hold naval-officer ranks and are usually stationed on vanguard ships, in both the PanOceanian Armada and spacecraft belonging to the Order itself. Because the Order's relationship with the Navy sees Knights assuming posts of great responsibility in naval ships and bases, the Knights of Santiago are considered to be the technically best trained members of the Military Orders.

Boarding Operations

The members of the Order of Santiago are some of the best trained and most specialized troops in the Human Sphere. The operational role of its boarding units is to storm, seize, and secure spaceships and facilities, both in deep space and in planetary orbits. This type of operation always drives them to the front line. The Knights must carry out insertions at high speed, following assault vectors that exploit the smallest gaps in the point-defense systems of the enemy ships. Crammed into the acceleration seats of their boarding pods, they are subjected to hours of pain and fatigue caused by the high-velocity maneuvers required to reach their target while avoiding incoming defensive gunfire.

Once they reach the enemy spaceship's hull and manage to breach it, the Knights of Santiago must fight their way through compartments and airlocks to seize and secure each of the ship's sections, fiercely fighting in close quarters, tooth and nail, against their enemies. These actions are generally death traps but also the fastest and most effective way to board a ship, and therefore the Order's favored choice. The Knights know that their patron saint, Apostle James the Great, favors and always rewards risky enterprises when they are undertaken with Faith and the most indomitable courage.

Organization

The Grand Master of the Order has no single orbital that could be considered a fixed headquarters, instead moving continuously from one to another, while his second, called the Attorney, performs a parallel journey on other ships and orbitals. The paths of both intersect on Earth in the Episcopal Palace of Santiago of Compostela, where the main ceremonies of the Order are held.

The Knights of Santiago see their spacecraft as mobile monasteries and their internal organization is developed along these lines, with the ranks of the Order revealing this special conception of their fleet. A Prior-Captain is in charge of a mid-sized attack ship, while an Abbot-Commodore is responsible for a mothership or a fleet of ships. The Pilot-Officers, either of ships or TAGs, are called Brethren of Spirit, and the Father-Officers are simply Brother Knights, or just Knights. Certain positions within ships receive names that combine Church and naval terms (Father-Navigator, Father-Gunner, and so on) to avoid confusion with posts shared with the PanOceanian Navy.

Order of Santiago Facilities
Tencendur

The system with the largest number of Order of Santiago facilities is Tencendur, home to planet Neoterra, the capital of PanOceania, and the origin and destination point of most of PanOceanian space traffic. In addition to Potestas Station near the system's main Jump Gate, which handles law-enforcement tasks such as patrols and cargo inspection, the Order also has a fortress-monastery orbiting around Aphrodite to prevent illegal mining and smuggling on the planet. Suseia Station, on the other hand, is located in orbit on the dark side of planet Hermes, providing protection and support to the various stations scattered across the infernal surface of this planet, the closest to the star. All members of the Order spend some time here during their training period, and it's considered the worst posting an acolyte of Santiago can be sent to, in addition to being the usual punishment for those who fail to live up to the high standards of the Order. Besides these locations, the Order also has its own docks at Eryx Station over planet Poseidon, an orbital shipyard for repairs and maintenance of the fleet that patrols the outer area of the system, or for spaceships arriving or about to go through the Jump Gate to other points of the Human Sphere. The Order also assists in security tasks at the Neoterra Orbital Astroport, as well as in the orbital defense system of this capital planet.

Descoberta

The second system with most space facilities is Descoberta, where the Order took on the mission of protecting the outer borders of Acontecimento by deploying a series of stations and Mobases (mobile orbital bases) throughout the system, including the orbit of each of its planets.

Human Edge

Similarly, the Order has several smaller orbital fortresses in Human Edge spread out in relevant positions, and their operations are coordinated from Ulloa Station, named after Commodore-Abbot Ulloa, a hero of the NeoColonial Wars.

Mitra

Of note in the Mitra System is the Sword-Cross-class fortress-monastery Sancti Spiritus orbiting Durga, the sixth planet of the system, so located to keep a close watch on the traffic heading to Varuna from the outer reaches of the system and from the Jump Gates.

The Historical Order

Santiago was founded in the kingdom of Leon in 1170.

First difference: where the rest took the monastic vows of poverty, chastity and obedience whole, Santiago required only conjugal chastity. Married men could be full members and keep their wives. To quote Corinthians, "it is better to marry than to burn."

It was not uncontroversial, thus Pope Alexander III took five years to approve the order at all. It's understandable, as the Church had spent a century trying to enforce clerical celibacy. Still, the concession was fenced in tightly. Married brethren lived outside the convents with their families but had to leave them and move inside during fasts, their wives going to a sisters' house for the duration. Unmarried members needed the master's permission to marry.

The order's other distinguishing commitment was ransom. Santiago and the small order of Mountjoy pursued the release of Christian prisoners far more consistently than the Hospital did, and between the late twelfth and early thirteenth centuries Santiago built around ten ransom hospitals across Leon, Castile and Aragon. Several towns, Cuenca and Huete and Moya among them, paid it an annual benefaction for the purpose. Authority sat with a council of thirteen leading commanders called the Trece, and it was the Trece that chose the master.
